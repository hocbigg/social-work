# Great Courses

A curated directory of standout full-length university lectures, high-production open courses, and specialized audiovisual masterclasses designed to build deep clinical intuition, policy acumen, and community leadership in Social Work.

## Clinical Practice, Trauma & Mental Health

- [Psychological First Aid (Coursera / Johns Hopkins University / Dr. George S. Everly Jr.)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/psychological-first-aid)
- [The Social Context of Mental Health and Illness (Coursera / University of Toronto / Dr. Charmaine Williams)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/mental-health)
- [Addiction Treatment: Clinical Skills for Healthcare Providers (Coursera / Yale University / Dr. Ellen Edens)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/addiction-treatment)
- [Palliative Care Always Specialization (Coursera / Stanford University / Dr. Kavitha Ramchandran)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/palliative-care-always)
- [Understanding Violence (Coursera / Emory University / Dr. Deb Houry and Dr. Pamela Scully)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/understanding-violence)

## Macro Practice, Community Organizing & Program Evaluation

- [Community Organizing for Social Justice (Coursera / University of Michigan / Prof. Barry Checkoway)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/community-organizing)
- [Evaluating Social Programs (MIT OpenCourseWare / Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab / Prof. Esther Duflo and Prof. Abhijit Banerjee)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-14-001-abdul-latif-jameel-poverty-action-lab-executive-training-evaluating-social-programs-2009-spring-2009/)
- [Social Norms, Social Change I (Coursera / University of Pennsylvania and UNICEF / Prof. Cristina Bicchieri)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/norms)

## Child Welfare, Youth & Trauma-Informed Systems

- [Child Protection: Children's Rights in Theory and Practice (Harvard University / HarvardX / Prof. Jacqueline Bhabha)](https://pll.harvard.edu/course/child-protection-childrens-rights-theory-and-practice)
- [Children's Human Rights: An Interdisciplinary Introduction (Coursera / University of Geneva / Prof. Philip D. Jaffé)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/childrens-rights)
- [Social Work: Perspectives on Trauma and Wellness (edX / University of Alaska Fairbanks / Prof. LaVerne Demientieff)](https://www.edx.org/certificates/professional-certificate/alaskax-social-work-perspectives-on-trauma-and-wellness)

## Global Human Rights, Migration & Humanitarian Action

- [International Women's Health and Human Rights (Coursera / Stanford University / Prof. Anne Firth Murray)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/womens-health-human-rights)
- [Human Rights for Open Societies (Coursera / Utrecht University / Prof. Janneke Gerards and Prof. Antoine Buyse)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/human-rights-for-open-societies)
- [Humanitarian Response to Conflict and Disaster (Harvard University / HarvardX / Prof. Jennifer Leaning and Dr. Michael VanRooyen)](https://pll.harvard.edu/course/humanitarian-response-conflict-and-disaster)
