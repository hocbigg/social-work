# Summary

The Social Work curriculum is a **complete education in Social Work** using online materials.

## Organization

This repository is organized into three main components:

- **Core Curriculum** (this page): the foundational knowledge of the field;
- **[Advanced Topics](advanced_topics.md)**: focused study in specific areas;
- **[Projects](projects.md)**: support learning through practical application throughout the curriculum.

**Process:** Learners may work through the curriculum independently or collaboratively, and either sequentially or selectively.

- For simplicity, courses in the Core Curriculum are ordered according to their prerequisites.
- The Core Curriculum provides a shared foundation and is intended to be completed in full.
- Advanced Topics are optional; learners are encouraged to select one area of focus and complete all courses within that topic.

Practical work is integrated through the [Projects section](projects.md) and may be undertaken alongside coursework.

Note: When there are courses or books that don't fit into the curriculum but are otherwise of high quality,
they belong in [extras/courses](extras/courses.md) or [extras/readings](extras/readings.md).

# Curriculum

## Intro

| Subject | Why study? | Book | Videos |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction to Social Work | Provides foundational knowledge of the profession's history, values, and role in addressing social issues. | *Introduction to Social Work: An Advocacy-Based Profession* by Karen K. Kirst-Ashman & Grafton H. Hull (or similar recent edition) | [Introduction to Social Work (Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/introduction-to-social-work) |
| Social Work Values and Ethics | Establishes the ethical foundation, core values, and decision-making framework of the profession from the start. | *Social Work Values and Ethics* by Frederic G. Reamer | [Ethics in Social Work (edX)](https://www.edx.org/course/ethics-in-social-work) |
| Human Behavior in the Social Environment: Lifespan Development | Explains how individuals develop across the lifespan, informing interventions for diverse populations. | *Human Behavior and the Social Environment: Shifting Paradigms in Essential Knowledge for Social Work Practice* by Joe M. Schriver | [Human Development Across the Lifespan (edX)](https://www.edx.org/course/human-development-across-the-lifespan) |
| Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion in Social Work | Builds awareness of diversity, difference, oppression, and social justice to support anti-oppressive practice. | *Social Work and Social Welfare: An Introduction* by Marla Berg-Weger | [Diversity and Social Justice in Social Work (various platforms)] |
| Academic and Professional Writing | Enhances skills for clear communication in reports, grants, and policy briefs. | *The Social Work Portfolio* by Lee Ann Bartoshesky | [Academic English: Writing Specialization (Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/academic-english) |
| Critical Thinking and Evidence-Informed Practice | Equips practitioners to evaluate research and apply best practices ethically. | *Critical Thinking for Social Work* by Keith Brown and Sarah Sohrab | [Critical Thinking Skills for University Success (Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/critical-thinking-skills) |

## Core

| Subject | Why study? | Book | Videos |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human Behavior in the Social Environment: Theories | Offers frameworks to understand behavior in context, guiding holistic assessments. | *Human Behavior in the Social Environment* (open textbook or Elizabeth Hutchison series) | [Social Psychology (Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/social-psychology) |
| Social Welfare Policy: History and Frameworks | Contextualizes policies shaping social services, enabling informed advocacy. | *Social Welfare Policy and Advocacy* by Bruce S. Jansson | [American Government (edX)](https://www.edx.org/course/american-government) |
| Research Methods in Social Work | Builds skills for ethical, rigorous evaluation of interventions and programs. | *Research Methods for Social Work* by Allen Rubin and Earl R. Babbie | [Understanding Research Methods (Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/research-methods) |
| Social Welfare Policy: Analysis and Advocacy | Empowers policy critique and change efforts for equitable systems. | *The Policy-Based Profession* by Philip Popple and Leslie Leighninger | [Public Policy Challenges (edX)](https://www.edx.org/course/public-policy-challenges) |
| Generalist Social Work Practice: Overview | Introduces integrated, multi-level generalist framework before method-specific practice. | *Generalist Social Work Practice* (standard text e.g. Kirst-Ashman series) | [Social Work Practice (Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/social-work-practice) |
| Generalist Social Work Practice with Individuals and Families | Develops versatile skills for direct intervention across diverse settings. | *The Skills of Helping Individuals, Families, Groups, and Communities* by Felix Biestek | [Social Work Practice (Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/social-work-practice) |
