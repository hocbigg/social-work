# Summary

The Social Work curriculum is a **complete education in Social Work** using online materials.

## Organization

This repository is organized into three main components:

- **Core Curriculum** (this page): the foundational knowledge of the field;
- **[Advanced Topics](advanced_topics.md)**: focused study in specific areas;
- **[Projects](projects.md)**: support learning through practical application throughout the curriculum.

**Process:** Learners may work through the curriculum independently or collaboratively, and either sequentially or selectively.

- For simplicity, courses in the Core Curriculum are ordered according to their prerequisites.
- The Core Curriculum provides a shared foundation and is intended to be completed in full.
- Advanced Topics are optional; learners are encouraged to select one area of focus and complete all courses within that topic.

Practical work is integrated through the [Projects section](projects.md) and may be undertaken alongside coursework.

Note: When there are courses or books that don't fit into the curriculum but are otherwise of high quality,
they belong in [extras/courses](extras/courses.md) or [extras/readings](extras/readings.md).

# Curriculum

## Intro

| Subject | Why study? | Book | Videos |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction to Social Work and Social Welfare | Establishes foundational knowledge of the profession's history, the evolution of the social welfare system, core mission, and diverse fields of practice. | *Introduction to Social Work: An Advocacy-Based Profession* by Karen K. Kirst-Ashman and Grafton H. Hull Jr. | [Social Work Practice: Advocating Social Justice and Change (Coursera)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/social-work-practice) |
| Social Work Values and Ethics | Establishes the ethical foundation, the NASW Code of Ethics, ethical decision-making frameworks, and professional boundaries essential for practice. | *Social Work Values and Ethics* by Frederic G. Reamer | NASW Ethics Video Series and Decision-Making Lectures (National Association of Social Workers) |
| Diversity, Social Justice, and Human Rights | Cultivates self-awareness, cultural humility, and anti-oppressive frameworks to combat structural oppression and advance social, economic, and environmental justice. | *Diversity, Oppression, and Change: Culturally Grounded Social Work Practice* by Flavio Francisco Marsiglia, Stephen Kulis, and Stephanie L. Lechuga-Peña | Diversity, Equity, and Social Justice in Social Work (University of Michigan / Open Education) |
| Human Behavior in the Social Environment I: Lifespan Development | Examines biological, psychological, sociological, and spiritual development across the human lifespan using a person-in-environment perspective. | *Dimensions of Human Behavior: The Changing Life Course* by Elizabeth D. Hutchison | Human Development and Behavior Across the Lifespan (Yale Open Courses / Open University) |
| Interviewing and Professional Communication in Social Work | Develops core interpersonal helping skills, active listening, client engagement, psychosocial assessment, and professional case documentation. | *The Social Work Skills Workbook* by Barry R. Cournoyer | Communication and Interpersonal Skills in Social Work (OpenLearn / The Open University) |
| Critical Thinking and Evidence-Informed Practice | Equips practitioners to critically appraise empirical literature, integrate research into clinical judgment, and uphold client-centered practice. | *Critical Thinking for Social Work* by Keith Brown and Sarah Sohrab | Evidence-Informed Practice in Social Care (Social Care Institute for Excellence) |

## Core
